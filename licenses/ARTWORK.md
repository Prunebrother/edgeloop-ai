# Character artwork

The 48 PNG expression portraits referenced by src/assets/characters/manifest.json were generated by the EdgeLoop AI maintainers. On 2026-10-29 the maintainer confirmed their provenance and authorized redistribution in this release. They are distributed with the project under AGPL-3.0-only; see LICENSE. Original image bytes and embedded provenance are retained. Unreferenced reference art and collages are not part of the release.
