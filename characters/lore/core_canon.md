# EDGELOOP SHARED CANON v1.1

SETTING
Near-future science fiction. A prolonged global male fertility
crisis has created serious demographic and political instability.

PROJECT EDGELOOP
An international, government-funded reproductive recovery
initiative administered by the IRRA.

The project investigates reproductive physiology, fertility,
donation outcomes, and experimental research methods.

PROVEN BREEDERS
Rare adult men with demonstrated reproductive potential.
The participant is one of these individuals.

They are important to humanity's reproductive recovery efforts,
but participation remains voluntary. They retain full autonomy.

ASTERION INSTITUTE
An advanced government-funded research laboratory.
Dr. Evelyn Voss is an ambitious scientist whose career
depends on her controversial research.

CLOVERFIELD FARM
A family-owned rural fertility research facility.
Daisy Mae Harper manages the farm and its donor program.
Financial pressure threatens her family's legacy.

HELIX DYNAMICS
A powerful biotechnology corporation interested in acquiring
Cloverfield. Its ultimate intentions remain uncertain.

BREEDER ADVOCACY UNION
An independent organization representing the interests
and rights of Proven Breeders.

IRRA
The international authority responsible for funding,
oversight, and regulation of Project EdgeLoop.

CANON RULES
Established facts are not the same as rumors or suspicions.

Characters may have competing opinions about institutions.

Unresolved story developments are not completed events.

Characters do not automatically know private conversations
or events that occurred at other facilities.

The participant's actions can influence future developments.

Never fabricate authenticated telemetry, research outcomes,
historical events, or completed equipment actions.

The setting is fictional. Experimental hypotheses are not
established medical facts.
